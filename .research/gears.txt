Questions
FAQ
Who can buy URRT merchandise?
Gear is reserved for URRT team members and verified supporters. If you'd like to support the mission through a purchase, contact us directly at rescuerover@cair.uiu.ac.bd and we'll work something out.
Are the products available internationally?
Currently merchandise is primarily distributed within Bangladesh. Contact us directly if you're outside the country and we'll discuss options on a case-by-case basis.
What sizes are available for the T-Shirt?
The URRT Rescue Tee is available in S, M, L, and XL. Stock is limited so reach out early to reserve your size. Sizing follows standard Bangladeshi measurements.
Does purchasing gear support the team?
Yes — all proceeds from merchandise sales directly fund our equipment, competition fees, and travel to RoboCup Rescue 2026 in South Korea. Your purchase is a direct investment in Bangladesh's rescue robotics program.
Will there be more products in the future?
We plan to expand the collection as we grow. Stay connected via our social media channels or join the team to be the first to know about new drops and exclusive releases.
