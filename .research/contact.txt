FAQ
COMMON QUESTIONS
Quick answers to the questions we receive most often.
How can I join the URRT team?
We recruit passionate students from UIU across mechanical, electrical, and software engineering disciplines. Visit our
Join Us
page to learn about open roles and apply. You can also email us directly with your CV and a short motivation letter.
How can my company sponsor URRT?
We offer tiered sponsorship packages ranging from equipment support to title sponsorship. In return, sponsors receive visibility on our rover, website, competition materials, and media coverage. Contact us at rescuerover@cair.uiu.ac.bd with the subject "Sponsorship" to receive our sponsorship prospectus.
What competitions is URRT preparing for?
Our primary target is RoboCup Rescue 2026, to be held in Icheon, South Korea in July 2026. This is the world's premier competition for urban search and rescue robotics, attracting teams from 40+ countries.
Can external researchers collaborate with URRT?
Yes! We welcome collaboration with researchers, universities, and industry professionals in the fields of robotics, AI, and autonomous systems. Please reach out via the contact form above or email us directly to discuss potential collaborations.
How long does it take to get a response?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on working days (Sunday–Thursday). For urgent matters, you can call us directly at +880 1909 356 346 between 9AM and 5PM BST.
